# AEO: Answer Engine Optimization

Goal: be the clearest, most trustworthy answer to a specific question.

## Method
1. **Collect questions** customers really ask: PAA, autocomplete, sales calls, support tickets, Reddit, communities.
2. **Answer-first formatting:** place a direct 40-60 word answer right under a question-style heading, then expand.
3. Match the format to the question: definition paragraph, numbered steps for how-tos, bullet lists, comparison tables.
4. **One question per heading**, plain words, conversational phrasing.
5. Add FAQ sections with concise answers; keep them genuinely useful.
6. Use clear entities and terminology; define terms on first use.
7. Add relevant schema and keep content fresh and accurate.
8. Build topical depth so engines trust the whole site on the subject.
9. For voice queries: short, natural, speakable answers; strengthen local info if the query is local.

## Output
Question list with priority, recommended page/section, draft answer block, and format.
