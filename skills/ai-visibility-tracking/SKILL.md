# AI Visibility Tracking

1. Build a **prompt set** of 30-100 real customer questions across funnel stages (problem, comparison, "best X for Y", brand, pricing).
2. Run each prompt on ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude and Google AI Overviews, multiple times because answers vary. Use logged-out or clean sessions and record date and country.
3. Record: brand mentioned (Y/N), position in the answer, sentiment, sources cited, competitors named, accuracy of what is said.
4. Compute: **mention rate**, **citation rate**, **share of voice vs competitors**, sentiment, and error rate.
5. Check referral traffic in analytics from AI platforms (e.g. chatgpt.com, perplexity.ai) and add "how did you hear about us" on forms.
6. Fix inaccuracies by correcting source pages and strengthening authoritative profiles.
7. Repeat monthly and trend the results. Dedicated tools exist; verify their methodology.

Output: dashboard table, key wins and losses, and 5 next actions. Be clear that sample sizes are small and results are directional.
