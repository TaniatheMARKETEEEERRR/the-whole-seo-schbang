# Brand Voice Guide

## Build
From the intake and sample content, define: voice adjectives, reader we talk to, point of view (we/you), reading level, preferred and banned words, formatting habits, humor level, stance on claims, and 3 do/don't example sentences.

## Enforce
Review a draft against the guide. Flag off-voice sentences, inconsistent terminology (product names, capitalization), and risky claims (guarantees, superlatives, competitor comparisons without proof).

Output: one-page voice guide, or a review table (Line | Issue | Suggested fix).
