# Competitor Analysis

1. **Identify** true search competitors (who ranks for your target keywords), which may differ from business competitors. Include publishers, marketplaces and communities.
2. **Content audit:** top pages, topics covered, formats, depth, update frequency, author credibility.
3. **Keyword gap:** keywords they rank for that you do not; where you are close (positions 4-20).
4. **Authority:** notable backlinks, digital PR, brand mentions, reviews.
5. **Technical glance:** site structure, speed, schema used, internal linking patterns.
6. **AI visibility:** ask ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google AI Overviews 10-20 customer questions. Record which brands and sources are cited and what content type earns citations.
7. **Synthesize:** strengths to match, weaknesses to exploit, unique angles.

## Output
Competitor comparison table, top 10 content gaps ranked by opportunity, and a 90-day action list. Cite where each data point came from and mark anything unverified.
