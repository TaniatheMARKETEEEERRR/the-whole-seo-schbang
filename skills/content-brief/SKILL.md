# Content Brief

Use `templates/content-brief-template.md` as the structure.

## Steps
1. Confirm primary keyword, intent and audience.
2. Review the top results: note structure, depth, what they miss.
3. Write an **answer-first summary**: the direct answer in 40-60 words that opens the page (helps featured snippets and AI answers).
4. Outline with question-style H2/H3 headings where natural.
5. List the **original value** the page must add: first-hand experience, data, examples, expert quote, visuals. Without this, the page is just a rewrite of what exists.
6. Specify sources to cite, internal links, schema type, CTA, and word range (driven by the topic, not a fixed number).

## Output
A completed brief ready to hand to the `content-writer-agent`.
