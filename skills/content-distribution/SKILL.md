# Content Distribution

1. Repurpose each piece: LinkedIn post, carousel, short video script, newsletter section, social thread, sales enablement snippet.
2. Share where the audience gathers: LinkedIn, relevant communities, Reddit and forums (follow rules, add value, disclose affiliation), newsletters, partner audiences.
3. Pitch to journalists and creators when you have original data.
4. Encourage employees and experts to share with their own take.
5. Revisit top posts monthly and republish updates.
6. Track referral traffic, mentions and any new backlinks.

Output: a 2-week distribution plan per piece with channel, format, copy draft, and timing.
