# Ecommerce SEO

- **Category pages** are the main targets: unique intro, helpful buying guide copy, clean filters, internal links to top products.
- **Product pages:** unique descriptions, specs, FAQs, real reviews, `Product` schema with accurate price and availability, multiple quality images with alt text.
- **Faceted navigation:** control crawl bloat with canonicals, noindex or robots rules; allow only valuable filter combos to index.
- **Out of stock / discontinued:** keep, redirect, or mark appropriately.
- **Feeds:** keep Google Merchant Center data accurate.
- **Content:** buying guides, comparisons, "best X for Y" pages.
- **Speed and mobile** are critical for conversion.

Output: store-specific audit and prioritized fixes.
