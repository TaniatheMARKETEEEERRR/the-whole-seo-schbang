# E-E-A-T and Content Quality Review

Check and score each 1-5, with fixes:
- **Experience:** first-hand details, photos, process, results, mistakes learned.
- **Expertise:** named author with relevant credentials, bio page, expert review for sensitive topics.
- **Authoritativeness:** reputable citations, mentions, links from respected sources.
- **Trust:** accurate and sourced claims, clear business info, contact details, policies, reviews, secure site, transparent about ads/affiliates.
- **Helpfulness:** written for people first; fully satisfies the query; no padding; original value beyond rewriting the top results.
- **Risk:** for health, finance or legal topics (YMYL), require expert review and conservative claims.

Note: E-E-A-T is a quality framework, not a single ranking score. Present it as guidance.
