# Fact-Checking

1. Extract every checkable claim: statistics, dates, names, quotes, product features, legal or medical statements, "best" or "first" claims.
2. For each, find a primary or reputable source. Confirm the number, date and context match.
3. Label each: **Verified** (source link), **Outdated** (newer data exists), **Unsupported** (no source found), **Incorrect**.
4. Fix: replace with sourced fact, soften ("often", "in our experience"), or delete.
5. Check quotes are real and attributed correctly. Never fabricate quotes, studies, reviews or case studies.
6. Check links work and point to the right page.
7. Flag YMYL content (health, finance, legal) for expert human review.

Output: claims table (Claim | Status | Source | Fix).
