# GEO: Generative Engine Optimization

Nobody can guarantee citations. Focus on increasing the probability by being easy to retrieve, easy to quote and credible.

## Principles
1. **Be quotable:** self-contained passages (2-4 sentences) that make sense when lifted out. Lead sections with clear claims.
2. **Add evidence:** original data, statistics with sources, named experts, case studies. Fact-dense, well-sourced content tends to be cited more.
3. **Entity clarity:** consistent brand name, description, category and founders across the site, LinkedIn, Crunchbase, Wikipedia/Wikidata (if eligible), directories and Google Business Profile. Use `Organization` schema and `sameAs`.
4. **Third-party presence:** AI systems lean on what others say. Pursue reviews, listicles, comparisons, podcasts, Reddit/Quora discussions (be transparent, no spam), industry publications and digital PR.
5. **Cover comparison and "best X for Y" queries** honestly, with clear criteria.
6. **Freshness:** update key pages and show real update dates.
7. **Crawl access:** make sure the right AI crawlers and search bots can reach key pages; ensure content is in HTML, not hidden behind scripts or logins.
8. **Clear structure:** headings, tables, definitions, summaries, FAQs.
9. Optionally publish an `llms.txt` file as a low-effort experiment, but note that major providers have not confirmed it affects results.
10. Ranking well in traditional search still feeds many AI answers, so SEO fundamentals remain the base.
