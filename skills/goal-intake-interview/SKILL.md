# Goal Intake Interview

Never start SEO work without a clear goal. Ask in small batches (3-4 questions at a time), in plain language, and skip anything already answered.

## Questions
**Goal:** What outcome matters most: leads, sales, signups, calls, brand awareness, hiring, or authority? What number would make you call this a success in 3, 6 and 12 months?
**Business:** What do you sell, to whom, in which regions and languages? What is your average deal value and sales cycle?
**Starting point:** Do you have a website, analytics, Search Console, existing content, existing rankings? Any past penalties or migrations?
**Resources:** Monthly budget, who writes and publishes, developer access, tools you already pay for.
**Competition:** Who do you see as competitors? Who do you admire?
**AI search:** Do customers already ask ChatGPT or Perplexity about your category? Do you want to appear there?
**Constraints:** Legal or compliance limits (health, finance, legal claims), brand rules, topics to avoid, deadlines.
**Voice:** Three adjectives for your brand voice and an example of writing you like.

## Output: the Goal Brief
One page: primary goal, success metrics, audience, scope (SEO / AEO / GEO / local), resources, constraints, assumptions, and open questions. Ask the user to confirm it before handing off to `seo-orchestrator`.
