# Humanizer Editing

Purpose: improve quality and readability, not to trick detectors. Keep claims truthful and follow any disclosure rules your publication or industry requires.

## Edit pass
1. **Cut filler openers and closers:** "In today's digital landscape", "In conclusion", "It's important to note".
2. **Remove overused AI words and patterns:** delve, tapestry, game-changer, unlock, leverage, seamless, robust, navigate the landscape, "not just X, but Y" repeated, and rule-of-three lists everywhere.
3. **Vary rhythm:** mix short and long sentences; use contractions where the brand voice allows.
4. **Add specifics:** replace vague claims with a number, name, example or short story. Ask the user for real details if none exist, never invent them.
5. **Add a point of view:** opinions, trade-offs, what to skip, what you would actually do.
6. **Use concrete nouns and active verbs.** Trim adverbs and hedging.
7. **Cut repetition** and paragraphs that restate the previous one.
8. **Reduce em-dash and bullet overuse;** write connected prose where logic flows.
9. **Read aloud test:** if a person would not say it, rewrite it.
10. Preserve keywords, facts, links and structure unless they hurt readability.

## Output
The edited text plus a short change log (what patterns were fixed, what needs human input such as real anecdotes or data).
