# Internal Linking

1. Crawl the site; list pages with inlinks count, depth and traffic.
2. Find **orphan pages** (no inlinks) and priority pages with few inlinks.
3. For each priority page, find 3-10 relevant source pages with traffic or authority and suggest a contextual link.
4. Anchor text: descriptive and varied, not "click here", not exact-match stuffed.
5. Follow the topical map: cluster pages link to the pillar and to each other where relevant.
6. Add links high in content where natural. Fix broken links and redirect chains.

Output: Source URL | Target URL | Suggested anchor | Sentence where it fits.
