# Keyword Research

## Inputs needed
Business context (offer, customer, region, goal). If missing, ask for it first.

## Process
1. **Seed topics:** list 5-10 core themes from the product, customer problems and competitor sites.
2. **Expand** each seed into: problems ("why is..."), solutions ("how to..."), comparisons ("X vs Y", "alternatives"), buying terms ("pricing", "best", "for [industry]"), and questions.
3. **Collect signals** (use tools the user has: Search Console, Semrush, Ahrefs, Google Keyword Planner, autocomplete, People Also Ask, Reddit/Quora). Never invent search volumes. If no data is available, label estimates as "unverified".
4. **Classify** every keyword by intent and funnel stage.
5. **Prioritize** with a score: business value (1-5) x realistic ability to rank (1-5) / effort (1-5).
6. **Cluster** keywords that one page can satisfy together; one primary keyword per page.

## Output
A table: Keyword | Intent | Funnel stage | Volume (source) | Difficulty (source) | Priority | Suggested page type | Cluster.
End with the top 10 quick wins and 5 long-term bets.

## Rules
Prefer topics tied to revenue over vanity traffic. Flag keywords where AI answers or SERP features may reduce clicks, and suggest AEO/GEO angles there.
