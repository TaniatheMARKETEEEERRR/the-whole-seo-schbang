# Local SEO

1. **Google Business Profile:** correct primary category, services, hours, description, photos, products, Q&A answers, posts; verify the listing.
2. **NAP consistency:** same name, address, phone everywhere.
3. **Citations:** relevant directories and data aggregators; fix duplicates.
4. **Reviews:** ask every happy customer, reply to all reviews, never fake or gate them.
5. **Location pages:** unique content per location/service (staff, photos, local proof, directions, FAQs), with `LocalBusiness` schema and embedded map.
6. **Local content and links:** local partners, news, events, sponsorships.
7. **On-site:** city + service in titles and headings naturally; click-to-call; mobile-first.
8. Track: calls, direction requests, GBP views, local pack rankings.

Output: prioritized checklist with the fixes for this business.
