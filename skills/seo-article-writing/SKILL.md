# SEO Article Writing

## Before writing
Need a brief (create one with `content-brief` if missing) and the brand voice.

## Structure
- **Title:** clear and specific, primary keyword near the front, no clickbait.
- **Intro (max 120 words):** answer the main question right away, then say what the reader will get.
- **Body:** descriptive H2/H3s. Each section opens with a 1-2 sentence direct answer, then supporting detail, example or evidence.
- **Scannability:** short paragraphs, lists and tables where they genuinely help.
- **Original value:** at least one thing a competitor page lacks (real example, data, process, expert view).
- **FAQ:** 3-6 real questions with concise answers.
- **CTA:** one relevant next step.

## Writing rules
- Plain language, specific nouns, active voice. Vary sentence length.
- No filler openers ("In today's fast-paced world"), no keyword stuffing, no invented statistics or quotes. Every claim that is a fact needs a source or should be softened.
- Use the keyword and related terms naturally.
- Do not add generic AI phrasing ("delve", "game-changer", "unlock", "tapestry").
- Mark anything needing human verification as [VERIFY].

## Finish
Run `on-page-optimization` and `eeat-content-quality` checks, then supply meta title, description, slug and suggested internal links.
