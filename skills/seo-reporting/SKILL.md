# SEO Reporting

## Metrics
- **Visibility:** impressions, average position, share of voice, AI mention and citation rates.
- **Traffic:** organic clicks and sessions by landing page and by topic cluster.
- **Engagement:** engaged sessions, scroll, time, conversion paths.
- **Business outcomes:** leads, signups, sales, pipeline and revenue attributed to organic (and AI referrals).
- **Health:** indexed pages, Core Web Vitals, errors.
- **Authority:** referring domains gained, quality mentions, reviews.

## Report structure
1. One-paragraph executive summary in plain language.
2. Headline numbers vs previous period and previous year.
3. What worked and why. What did not and why.
4. Wins explained with the action that caused them (when it can be reasonably supported).
5. Next 30-day plan with owners.

Rules: do not report vanity metrics without business context; distinguish correlation from causation; state data sources and date ranges; keep it under two pages.
